Syntax & Data Entry

Matrix cells accept integers (-3), decimals (1.25), or fractions (-3/4). Vectors are comma-separated lists like 1, -3/4, 2 in 2D or 3D.

Algebraic & calculus fields accept + − * / ^, implicit multiplication (3x, 2(x+1)), constants pi and e, and functions sin, cos, tan, asin, acos, atan, sinh, cosh, tanh, ln, log (base 10), sqrt, abs, exp. Complex input boxes also evaluate expressions like pi/4 or sqrt(2).

Scientific Calculator (8 Modes & Engine Bridges)

Module 09 uses a zero-eval recursive-descent AST engine across eight modes: Standard, Scientific (DEG/RAD, 2nd functions, ALPHA registers A–C, X–Y, combinatorics), Engineering (SI prefixes T–p, CODATA constants, par(), db10(), db20()), Complex (rectangular & r∠θ polar with → Complex Lab), and Fractions (mixed 2 3/4, improper, continued fraction, prime factors).

Specialized modes include Statistics (1D quartiles/SD/SEM & 2D x:y OLS regression with MXGraph plot), Programmer (exact 64/32/16/8-bit BigInt HEX/DEC/OCT/BIN with interactive clickable bit register), and Equations (f(x)=g(x) root finder, degree 1–4 polynomials, and exact RREF Ax=b solver with Matrix Lab handoff).

SI Units, Prefixes & Angle Modes

Complex Numbers and the Scientific Calculator support switching freely between radians and degrees for trigonometry and polar outputs.

Electrical Engineering provides explicit SI selectors (Ω/kΩ/MΩ, μF/nF/F, mH/μH/H, Hz/kHz, ms/s) and formats outputs cleanly in engineering SI prefixes. Mechanics uses SI defaults (N, kN, m, mm, kg, m/s², degrees), and Fluids enforces consistent SI units (kg/m³, Pa/kPa/bar/atm, m/s, Pa·s).

Exact Arithmetic vs. Numerical Approximations

Matrix Algebra (except irrational eigenvalues), Vector Algebra, and pure rational Scientific Calculator expressions run on exact BigInt rationals. Differentiation and Integration apply symbolic rules and cross-check closed-form antiderivatives numerically at sample points.

Any numerical method—such as Trapezoidal/Simpson integration rules, irrational eigenvalues, De Moivre non-integer powers, RLC phasors, transcendental Scientific Calculator functions, or Colebrook-White Darcy friction iteration—is explicitly badged as a numerical approximation alongside its assumptions.

Input Validation, Refusals & Engineering Warnings

MATHNEX never guesses around invalid mathematics or impossible physics. Singular matrices reject inversion, ∫ sin(x²) dx reports unsupported rather than fabricating a closed form, scientific expressions catch mismatched brackets, division by zero, and domain errors, and unphysical parameters are refused with corrective guidance.

Non-fatal engineering advisories—such as Bernoulli cavitation risk (P₂_abs < 2.34 kPa) or transitional pipe flow (2300 ≤ Re ≤ 4000)—are flagged with high-contrast warning callouts.

ABOUT THE WORKSPACE

About MATHNEX

MATHNEX grew from MATRIXX, a focused matrix calculator, into a fourteen-module engineering learning, mathematics, computing, materials, and textile science workspace. It helps students and engineers inspect derivations step by step, run Python 3 programs, test stress–strain behavior, analyze textile fibres, and recognize model boundaries—completely locally, with no accounts, tracking, or locked features.

Responsible-Use & Engineering Verification Notice

MATHNEX is an educational calculation and visualization aid built on idealized models (exact rational algebra, Euler-Bernoulli mechanics, lumped LTI circuits, steady 1D hydraulics, a browser-based Python 3 runtime, illustrative constitutive curves, and schematic fibre illustrations). Every calculated output must be independently verified against official course syllabi, reference standards, governing codes, and engineering judgment before use in graded coursework or design.
